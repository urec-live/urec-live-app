Privacy policy

Your workouts are personal. This policy explains what UREC Live collects, why it is used, and how you can request deletion.

Last updated: November 8, 2026. This policy covers the UREC Live app, its supporting services, and this privacy website. UREC Live is responsible for the data described here. Contact: mdasthanmay@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account and profile
Email address, username, account identifier, account creation date, access roles, profile visibility and any biography you provide. For password sign-in, we store a password hash. Google sign-in supplies your email and Google account identifier; we do not receive your Google password.
Workouts and plans
Exercises, sets, repetitions, weight lifted, duration, distance, workout times, notes, custom exercises and workout plans. We use these records to show history, progress, statistics and personal records.
Gym activity and support
If you connect to a gym, we process your gym connection, equipment check-ins and check-outs, staff-help requests, equipment issue descriptions and their status and timestamps.
Community features
When you use available social features, we process profile information, badges, leaderboard scores, community messages and direct messages, including participants and timestamps.
Technical information
Authentication tokens, locally cached app data and preferences help maintain your session and load your information. Our servers and hosting providers may process IP addresses, request details, timestamps and error or security logs to deliver and protect the service.
Privacy requests
If you contact us, we receive your email address, message and information needed to verify and resolve your request.

How we use information

We use information to create and authenticate accounts, save and display workouts, operate gym and community features you choose to use, respond to support requests, troubleshoot problems and protect accounts against misuse.

Camera and device storage

The QR scanner requests camera permission to read equipment codes. The scanner reads codes on your device; its scanning flow does not upload camera photos or video. You can revoke camera access in your device settings. The app stores session tokens and cached information on your device. Signing out clears account credentials and user-scoped cached resources. Deleting your account is a separate request.

Who can receive information

Service providers that host our app services and database process information needed to operate UREC Live. Google processes sign-in information when you choose Google sign-in. Authorized gym staff can access information needed to manage equipment and respond to help and issue reports. Other members can see information you choose to make public and messages you share with them. We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to address security threats.

Where GIF and sticker features are available, your searches are sent through our backend to KLIPY to retrieve results. Loading media from its delivery servers may disclose your IP address and request information to those servers. Shared media references, reactions and message reports are stored to provide chat and moderation features.

This privacy website uses no advertising or analytics scripts and loads no third-party fonts. Its hosting provider, Render, may process connection and request information to deliver and secure the website. Sending an email request also involves your email provider and ours.

Retention and deletion

Account information and associated records are retained while your account is active to provide the service. You can request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data through our account-deletion page. We complete verified requests within 30 days after verifying your request and email you when the process is complete.

Deletion covers your account credentials and profile, workout history and plans, gym associations, social records and messages, and personal identifiers in support reports and activity records. Equipment maintenance records may remain after your personal identifiers are removed. We also request deletion from service providers where applicable.

If a specific record must be retained for a legal obligation, security investigation or fraud prevention, we explain the record, reason and applicable retention period in our response, unless disclosure is legally restricted. We restrict retained information to that purpose. Backup copies may persist until the hosting provider’s backup rotation removes them; we explain the applicable period in our response and reapply deletion if a backup is restored.

Your choices and security

You can change your profile visibility, revoke camera access, sign out, or contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your information. Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply. We verify requests to protect your account. We use password hashing and authenticated access controls; no system can guarantee absolute security.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date when our practices change. For privacy questions or requests, email mdasthanmay@gmail.com. Do not send your password, sign-in codes or identity documents by email.
