Start your deletion request

You can request deletion without signing in or installing the app.

Request account deletion by email

Or email mdasthanmay@gmail.com with the subject UREC Live account deletion request.

The button opens your email app. Review and send the email to submit your request; clicking the button alone does not submit it. If no email app opens, use the address above in your usual email service.

What to include

  1. Send the request from the email address registered to your UREC Live account.
  2. Ask us to delete your account and associated personal data. Include your username if you know it.
  3. We verify account ownership by email before deletion. If you cannot access your registered email, contact us to discuss verification.

Never send your password, sign-in codes, payment information or identity documents.

What happens next

We complete deletion within 30 days after verifying your request and send a confirmation email. Deletion is permanent: your account and workout history cannot be recovered through the app. Uninstalling the app or signing out does not delete your account. Deleting UREC Live does not delete your Google account.

Data covered by your request

Your account credentials and profile; workout history, sets, notes and plans; gym associations; badges and leaderboard records; social messages; and personal identifiers in help requests, equipment reports and activity records are included. Equipment maintenance records may remain after your personal identifiers are removed. We request deletion from applicable service providers as well.

Possible exceptions

Records required for a legal obligation, security investigation or fraud prevention may be retained for that purpose. We tell you what is retained, why and for how long, unless legally restricted. Backup copies may remain until the hosting provider’s backup rotation removes them; we explain that period in our response. Copies of messages independently saved by other users are outside our control.

Read the privacy policy’s retention section for more information, or contact mdasthanmay@gmail.com with questions.
